# Shared components

- `components/news-card.tsx`: public article card linking to `/noticias/[slug]`.
- `components/news-list.tsx`: category filters, search and card grid.
- `components/article-view.tsx`: article hero, rich body, sharing and related cards.
- `components/ui/button.tsx`: shadcn-style Button with CVA variants.
- `components/ui/card.tsx`: shadcn-style Card primitives.
- `components/ui/input.tsx`, `textarea.tsx`, `label.tsx`, `badge.tsx`: form primitives.
- `components/admin/post-editor.tsx`: CMS form, Quill editor, banner and SEO panel.
