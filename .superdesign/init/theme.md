# Design system — Grupo +B / CMS

## Token summary
- Primary ink: `#120E0E`; charcoal: `#191616`; white: `#FDFAF6`; paper: `#F8F5F0`; beige: `#F3EFE8`.
- Brand gold: `#C89223`; legacy light gold: `#C49A45`; muted gray: `#8E8780`; dark gray: `#4D4845`.
- Typography: `DM Sans` for UI/body; `DM Serif Display` for editorial titles and accents.
- Radius: 6px small, 12px medium, 16–24px editorial cards; CMS uses 12px controls and 16px panels.
- Motion: 200–280ms ease; restrained hover lift; no gradients outside the legacy dark spotlight treatment.
- CMS surface: warm paper background, white panels, ink sidebar, gold active states, dense but breathable admin tables.
